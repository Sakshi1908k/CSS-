Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli (born 5 November 1988) is an Indian international cricketer. He is the former all-format captain of the Indian national cricket team. He is a right-handed batter and occasional right-arm medium-pace bowler. Considered one of the greatest batsmen in limited overs cricket, Kohli has been acclaimed for his batting skills and records. Kohli has the most centuries in ODIs and is one of the leading run-scorers in international cricket. He is also among the top run-scorers in the Indian Premier League.
